# StandBharat Product Specification

## 1. Product Overview
**Product Name:** StandBharat
**Category:** AI Marketing Operating System
**Core Concept:** An autonomous AI marketing team that researches a business, understands its brand, creates a marketing strategy, coordinates specialized AI agents, generates marketing work, routes risky actions through human approval, publishes through connected integrations, measures results, attributes revenue, and continuously recommends the next best marketing actions.

## 4. Key Systems
### AI CMO (Orchestrator)
The central intelligence layer capable of understanding business context, inspecting analytics, identifying opportunities, and orchestrating agents.

### Brand Brain
Structured knowledge system for product info, strategy, competitors, brand voice, and content strategy based on user inputs.

### Human-in-the-Loop (Approvals)
High-risk actions require explicit human approval before external publishing or modification.

### Analytics & Revenue Attribution
Integrates marketing events with revenue data (FIRST_TOUCH, LAST_TOUCH, LINEAR, TIME_DECAY).
